Dreamverse Academy

Project Presentation: Design Your Dream School

Durdona: Introduction & School Name

Hello, everyone! Today, we are going to present our dream school. Our school is called Dreamverse Academy.

The word “Dreamverse” means a universe of dreams. We chose this name because we believe that every student has a special talent and a unique dream.

Our school is not just a place where students study books and take exams. It is a place where they discover themselves, explore the world, and prepare for their future.

Motto: “Imagine it, learn it, create it!”

Our school has 25 modern classrooms. Each classroom has smart boards, computers, comfortable desks, and large windows. Students can sometimes choose where and how they want to study.

Nigina: Special Rooms

Future Lab: Here, students can learn about robots, artificial intelligence, space, and new technologies.

World Languages Room: Students can practise English and other languages by talking to students from different countries.

Creative Studio: Designed for drawing, photography, filmmaking, and designing clothes.

We also have a Science Lab, a music room, a library, and a sports centre.

The Dream Room: Our most unusual room where students share ideas, discuss goals, and turn dreams into real projects.

Dilnoza: Outdoor Areas and School Rules

Our school is surrounded by beautiful gardens and green trees. We have a football field, a basketball court, and an outdoor classroom.

Imagine studying biology in a garden instead of sitting in a classroom! Students can observe plants, learn about nature, and take care of the environment.

We also have a small café with healthy food and a quiet relaxation zone where students can read books or talk with friends.

School Rules: Students must respect teachers and classmates. Bullying is not allowed. Everyone must keep the school clean and help others.

Making mistakes is a natural part of learning. Students should not be afraid to ask questions or share opinions.

Muhammadaziz: Fun Activities

Future Careers Day: Try activities related to medicine, engineering, science, and design.

School of Ideas Competition: Work in teams to invent something useful and present it.

Culture Day: Monthly events discovering traditions, food, music, and languages from around the world.

Plus sports competitions, movie nights, art exhibitions, music shows, and exciting school trips!

Durdona: Conclusion & Uniqueness

What makes us different?

1. Real project-based learning rather than just textbooks.

2. Personal learning plans based on individual interests.

3. High focus on happiness, creativity, and confidence.

“Dreamverse Academy is more than a school. It is a place where imagination becomes reality.”

Thank you for listening! We hope you would love to study at Dreamverse Academy!
